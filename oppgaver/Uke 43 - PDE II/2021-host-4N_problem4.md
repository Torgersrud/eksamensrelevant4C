### **Problem 4** Wave equation [10 pts]

Consider the wave equation

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} - \frac{1}{4} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} = 0$$

on the real line $\mathbb{R}$. Use d'Alembert's solution formula to find the solution $u(x,t)$ satisfying initial conditions

$$\begin{aligned}u(x,0) &= x,\\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,0) &= \cos^2(x).\end{aligned}$$

Simplify the resulting expressions as much as possible.
