$$u_t(x,t)=6u_{xx}(x,t),\quad 0<x<\pi,\ t>0,$$
with boundary conditions
$$u(0,t)=u(\pi,t)=0,$$
and initial condition
$$u(x,0)=3\sin x+2\sin(2x)+\sin(3x).$$

a) Use the separation of variables to find the non-trivial solutions $u_n(x,t)$ to the heat equation with the given boundary conditions.

b) Use the initial condition to determine the full solution.

c) What is the behavior of $u(x,t)$ when $t\to\infty$. Explain briefly how this relate to the physical interpretation of the boundary conditions if we assume that these equations model a metal rod between $x=0$ and $x=\pi$ with temperature $u$?
