### **Problem 7** **Wave Equation [10 pts]**

The d'Alembert formula for solutions of the wave equation $u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}$ can be written in the form

$$u(t,x)=\frac{1}{2}(f(x+ct)+f(x-ct))+(g*h_t)(x),$$

where $h_t=1/(2c)$ on $[-ct,ct]$ and $w_t=0$ otherwise.

a) Express the Fourier transform $\hat u(t,w)$ of $u(t,x)$ with respect to the variable $x$ in terms of the Fourier transforms of $f$ and $g$.

b) Show that $v(t,w)=\hat u(t,x)$ satisfies the equation $v_{tt}=-c^2w^2v$ with the initial conditions $v(0,w)=\hat f(w)$ and $v_t(0,w)=\hat g(w)$.
