### **Solution to Problem 8** **Wave Equation [10 pts]**

The d'Alembert formula gives

$$u(t,x)=\frac{1}{2}(\sin(x+3t)+\sin(x-3t))+\frac{1}{6}\int_{x-3t}^{x+3t}\cos ydy.$$

We compute the integral and obtain

$$\begin{aligned}u(t,x)=\frac{1}{2}(\sin(x+3t)+\sin(x-3t))+\frac{1}{6}(\sin(x+3t)-\sin(x-3t))\\
=\frac{2}{3}\sin(x+3t)+\frac{1}{3}\sin(x-3t).\end{aligned}$$

**Grading manual** (3p) for d'Alembert's formula, (7p) for computing the integral and $u$.
