### **Problem 6** **Solving a PDE with Separation of Variables (14 points)**

We want to observe how the temperature $u$ of a heated wire evolves over time. This can be modelled by the following PDE
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=4\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2},\qquad\text{where}\quad 0<x<2\qquad\text{and}\quad t>0,$$
be given with boundary conditions
$$u(0,t)=u(2,t)=0\quad\text{where}\quad t\ge 0.$$

a) Classify the PDE and state its name if it has one.

b) Using separation of variables $u(x,t)=F(x)G(t)$ to show that all solutions of this PDE are of the following form
$$u_n(x,t)=e^{-\lambda_n t}\sin\Bigl(\frac{n\pi}{2}x\Bigr),\qquad n=1,2,\dots,$$
and state the value of $\lambda_n$.

c) Find the specific solution for the initial condition
$$u(x,0)=3\sin\bigl(\frac{7}{2}\pi x\bigr)+4\sin(5\pi x)\quad\text{where }0\le x\le 2.$$
Which principle do you have to use and why can you use it here?
