### **Problem 4** *TMA4135 Mathematics 4D:* [5 points]

Let $f,g$ be two smooth functions and let $c>0$ be a constant. Show that the function

$$u(x,t):=f(cx+t)+g(2cx-2t)+\sin cx\cos t$$

satisfies the wave equation $u_{xx}=c^2 u_{tt}$.
