### **Problem 4** (Wave equation)

Given is the wave equation on the real line

$$\partial_{tt} u(x,t) = 16 \partial_{xx} u(x,t), \quad x \in \mathbb{R},\, t > 0,$$

with initial conditions

$$u(x,0) = x, \quad \partial_t u(x,0) = \cos(x).$$

Find the solution to this problem, and simplify the resulting expression as much as possible.
