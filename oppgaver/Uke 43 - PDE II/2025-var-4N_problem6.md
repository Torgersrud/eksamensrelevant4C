### **Problem 6** **Solving a PDE with Separation of Variables (14 points)**

Let the following PDE
$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2}=4\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2},\qquad\text{where}\quad -1<x<1\quad\text{and}\quad t>0,$$
be given with boundary conditions
$$u(-1,t)=u(1,t)=0\quad\text{where}\quad t\ge 0.$$

a) Classify the PDE and state its name if it has one.

b) Using separation of variables $u(x,t)=F(x)G(t)$ to show that all solutions of this PDE are of the following two forms
$$u_n(x,t)=\bigl(A_n\cos(\lambda_n t)+B_n\sin(\lambda_n t)\bigr)\sin(\pi nx),\qquad n=1,2,\dots,$$
$$v_m(x,t)=\bigl(C_m\cos(\mu_m t)+D_m\sin(\mu_m t)\bigr)\cos\Bigl(\frac{\pi(2m-1)}{2}x\Bigr),\qquad m=1,2,\dots.$$
Find both $\lambda_n$ and $\mu_m$.

c) Find the specific solution for the initial conditions
$$u(x,0)=\sin(4\pi x)+3\sin(7\pi x)\qquad\text{and}\qquad\frac{\partial}{\partial t}u(x,0)=0,\qquad\text{where}\quad -1<x<1.$$
