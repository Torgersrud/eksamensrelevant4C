### **Problem 6** **Wave Equation [10 pts]**

We consider a string of length $L=1$ with its ends fixed at $x=0$ and $x=1$. The string is set to vibrate from rest by releasing it from an initial triangular shape $f(x)$:

[Figure: plot with arrowed vertical axis $u$ marked $0.1$ and arrowed horizontal axis $x$ marked $0$, $1/3$ and $1$. The graph, labelled $u(x,0)=f(x)$, is a triangle: a line segment rising from the origin to a peak just above the $0.1$ tick at $x=1/3$, then a line segment falling to $(1,0)$.]

a) Compute the coefficients $b_n$ and $b_n^*$ in the solution
$$u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sin\left(\frac{n\pi}{L}x\right)\left(b_n\cos(\lambda_nt)+b_n^*\sin(\lambda_nt)\right)$$
to the wave equation
$$u_{tt}=\frac{1}{\pi^2}u_{xx}.$$

b) Determine the $\lambda_n$.

c) Write down the final expression for $u(x,t)$.
