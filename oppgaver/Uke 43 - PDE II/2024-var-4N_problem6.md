### **Problem 6** **Separation of Variables (10 points)**

Find all non-trivial solutions of the equation
$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=3\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2},\qquad\text{where }-2\pi<x<2\pi,\quad\text{and }t>0,$$
that are of the form $u(x,t)=F(x)G(t)$ and satisfy the boundary conditions
$$u(-2\pi,t)=0\quad\text{and }\quad u(2\pi,t)=0,\qquad\text{for }t>0.$$
