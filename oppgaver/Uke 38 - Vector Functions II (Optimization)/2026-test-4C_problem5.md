### **Problem 5** (Gradient Descent, 10 points)

Consider the function

$$f(x,y)=x^{3}+7x^{2}-2xy+y^{2}+3x+6y.$$

a) Compute the gradient $\nabla f$ of $f$.

b) Perform two steps of the gradient descent method starting from the point $(x_0,y_0)=(0,1)$ with step size $s=0.1$.

c) What happens when you choose a step size $s=1$ instead in b)? What happens when you start at $(\tilde{x}_0,\tilde{y}_0)=(-1,-4)$?

d) Explain how we could use Newton's method here and state which further ingredient we would have to compute.

e) Why can we not start the method from d) at the point $(\tilde{x}_0,\tilde{y}_0)$ from c)?
