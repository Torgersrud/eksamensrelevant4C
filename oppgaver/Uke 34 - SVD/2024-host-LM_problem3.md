### **Problem 3**

Define

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&6\\ 5&3\\ 4&0\end{pmatrix}\qquad\text{and}\qquad b=\begin{pmatrix}1\\ 1\\ 2\end{pmatrix}.$$

a) Find the singular value decomposition of the matrix $A$.

b) Find $x\in\mathbb{R}^2$ such that $\lVert Ax-b\rVert_2$ is minimal.

*If you were unable to compute the SVD of $A$ in part a), you may instead solve part b) for the problem $\min_x\lVert Bx-b\rVert_2$, where the matrix $B\in\mathbb{R}^{3\times 2}$ has the singular value decomposition $B=U\Sigma V^{T}$ with $U=\frac{1}{3}\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\ 2&-2\\ 1&2\end{pmatrix}$, $\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\ 0&1\end{pmatrix}$, and $V=\frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}3&4\\ 4&-3\end{pmatrix}$. Do note that this is not the actual SVD of $A$!*
