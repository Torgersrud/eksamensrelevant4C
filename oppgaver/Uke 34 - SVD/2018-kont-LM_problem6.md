### **Problem 6**

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\ 2&0&-2\end{bmatrix}.$$

a) Find a singular value decomposition of $A$.

b) Find the pseudoinverse $A^{+}$ of $A$, and use it to find the minimal norm solution of the system:

$$x_1+0x_2+x_3=1$$
$$2x_1+0x_2-2x_3=4$$
