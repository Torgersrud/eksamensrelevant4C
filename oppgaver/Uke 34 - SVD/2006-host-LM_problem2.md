### **Problem 2**

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\ 1&1\\ 0&1\end{bmatrix}.$$

Find a singular value decomposition of $A$, and use the pseudo-inverse $A^{+}$ of $A$ to solve the least squares problem

$$Ax=\begin{bmatrix}2\\ 1\\ 2\end{bmatrix}.$$
