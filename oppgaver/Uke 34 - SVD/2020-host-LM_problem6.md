### **Problem 6**

*(In this problem you are expected to show full calculations, i.e. show how you arrive at the singular values and different decompositions of A.)*

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\ 2&-2\end{bmatrix}.$$

**a)** Determine if $A$ is normal. Find the singular values of $A$, and determine $\|A\|$ (the norm of the operator $\mathbb{C}^2\to\mathbb{C}^2$ represented by $A$).

**b)** Find a singular value decomposition of $A$.

**c)** Find the polar decomposition of $A$.
