### **Problem 4**

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}2&2\\ 2&2\\ -1&-1\end{bmatrix}.$$

a) Find a singular value decomposition of $A$.

b) Find the pseudoinverse $A^{+}$ of $A$, and use it to find the best approximation to a solution of the inconsistent system:

$$2x_1+2x_2=3$$
$$2x_1+2x_2=4$$
$$-x_1-x_2=-4$$
