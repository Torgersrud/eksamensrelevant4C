### **Problem 1** (Moore-Penrose inverse, 10 points)

Consider the matrix

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}-3&1\\ 1&-2\\ 1&3\\ 4&2\end{pmatrix}\in\mathbb{R}^{4\times 2}.$$

a) Compute its singular value decomposition $A=U\Sigma V^{\top}$ in reduced form.

b) Show that the Moore-Penrose inverse is given by

$$A^{\dagger}=\frac{1}{30}\begin{pmatrix}-4&2&0&4\\ 3&-4&5&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

c) Consider the linear system $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ with

$$\mathbf{b}=\begin{pmatrix}3\\ -3\\ 3\\ -3\end{pmatrix}.$$

Compute the vector $\mathbf{x}$ of smallest error in the linear system above and state the error.

d) What is the Moore-Penrose inverse of $A^{\top}$? Employ it to solve the linear system $A^{\top}\mathbf{y}=\mathbf{c}$ with

$$\mathbf{c}=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\ 6\end{pmatrix}$$

and interpret the result.
