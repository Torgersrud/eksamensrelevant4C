### **Problem 3**

Define

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}20&8&-2\\ 10&19&14\end{pmatrix}\qquad\text{and}\qquad b=\begin{pmatrix}12\\ -9\end{pmatrix}.$$

a) Find the singular value decomposition of the matrix $A$.

b) Find $x\in\mathbb{R}^3$ with $Ax=b$ such that $\lVert x\rVert_2$ is minimal.

*If you were unable to compute the SVD of $A$ in part a), you may instead solve part b) for the problem $Bx=b$, where the matrix $B\in\mathbb{R}^{2\times 3}$ has the singular value decomposition $B=U\Sigma V^{T}$ with $U=\frac{1}{5}\begin{pmatrix}4&-3\\ 3&4\end{pmatrix}$, $\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\ 0&1\end{pmatrix}$, and $V=\frac{1}{7}\begin{pmatrix}6&-2\\ 3&6\\ 2&-3\end{pmatrix}$. Do note that this is not the actual SVD of $A$!*
