### **Problem 1**

Let

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\ 2&3\\ 3&2\\ 4&1\end{pmatrix}\qquad\text{and}\qquad b=\begin{pmatrix}1\\ -1\\ 0\\ 0\end{pmatrix}.$$

a) Find the singular value decomposition of the matrix $A$.

b) Find $x\in\mathbb{R}^2$ such that $\lVert Ax-b\rVert_2$ is minimal.
