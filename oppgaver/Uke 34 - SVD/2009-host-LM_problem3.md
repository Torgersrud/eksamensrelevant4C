### **Problem 3**

A matrix $A$ has $QR$-factorisation:

$$\begin{bmatrix}1/2&1/2&1/2&1/2\\ 1/2&-1/2&1/2&-1/2\\ 1/2&1/2&-1/2&-1/2\\ 1/2&-1/2&-1/2&1/2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}8&-6&0\\ 0&0&5\\ 0&0&0\\ 0&0&0\end{bmatrix}$$

**1.** Explain why $A^{\top}A=R^{\top}R$ and compute this matrix. (2 points)

**2.** Find an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ of eigenvectors of $R^{\top}R$. (3 points)

**3.** Find the singular value decomposition of $R$ and hence find the singular value decomposition of $A$. (3 points)
