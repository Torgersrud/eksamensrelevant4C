### **Solution to Problem 1**

(a) The product $A^{H}A$ is

$$A^{H}A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3&4\\ 4&3&2&1\end{pmatrix}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\ 2&3\\ 3&2\\ 4&1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}30&20\\ 20&30\end{pmatrix}.$$

The eigenvalues of this matrix are the solutions to the equation $(30-\lambda)^{2}-(20)^{2}=0$, that is $\lambda_1=50$ and $\lambda_2=10$. Now,

$$\ker(A^{H}A-\lambda_1 I)=\ker\begin{pmatrix}-20&20\\ 20&-20\end{pmatrix}=\left[\begin{pmatrix}1\\ 1\end{pmatrix}\right],$$

that is, the line of $\mathbb{R}^2$ generated by the vector $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\end{pmatrix}^{T}$. Since we are interested in normalized vectors, we define

$$u_1:=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\ 1\end{pmatrix}$$

Similarly, for $\lambda_2=10$, we have that

$$\ker(A^{H}A-\lambda_2 I)=\ker\begin{pmatrix}20&20\\ 20&20\end{pmatrix}=\left[\begin{pmatrix}1\\ -1\end{pmatrix}\right],$$

and we define

$$u_2:=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix}1\\ -1\end{pmatrix}.$$

This defines one of the unitary matrices of the Singular Value Decomposition

$$P=(u_1|u_2)=\begin{pmatrix}1/\sqrt{2}&1/\sqrt{2}\\ 1/\sqrt{2}&-1\sqrt{2}\end{pmatrix}.$$

The singular values of $A$ are $\sigma_1=\sqrt{50}$ and $\sigma_2=\sqrt{10}$. And we define

$$v_1:=\frac{1}{\sigma_1}A(u_1)=\frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\ 1\\ 1\\ 1\end{pmatrix},\quad v_2:=\frac{1}{\sigma_2}A(u_2)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{20}}\begin{pmatrix}-3\\ -1\\ 1\\ 3\end{pmatrix}.$$

This gives rise to the other unitary matrix $Q=(v_1|v_2)$. We have the three matrices that define the Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) of $A:$

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-3/\sqrt{20}\\ 1/2&-1/\sqrt{20}\\ 1/2&1/\sqrt{20}\\ 1/2&3/\sqrt{20}\end{pmatrix},\quad\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt{50}&0\\ 0&\sqrt{10}\end{pmatrix},\quad P=\begin{pmatrix}1/\sqrt{2}&1/\sqrt{2}\\ 1/\sqrt{2}&-1\sqrt{2}\end{pmatrix}.$$

The SVD decomposition is

$$A=Q\circ\Sigma\circ P^{H}.$$

(b) It suffices to apply the formula $x=A^{\dagger}b$; where $A^{\dagger}$ is the Moore-Penrose inverse of $A:$

$$A^{\dagger}=P\circ\Sigma^{-1}\circ Q^{H}.$$

We first compute

$$Q^{H}\cdot b=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/2&1/2&1/2\\ -3/\sqrt{20}&-1/\sqrt{20}&1/\sqrt{20}&3/\sqrt{20}\end{pmatrix}\cdot b=\begin{pmatrix}1\\ -1\\ 0\\ 0\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\ -1/\sqrt{5}\end{pmatrix}.$$

We conclude

$$x=A^{\dagger}b=\begin{pmatrix}1/\sqrt{2}&1/\sqrt{2}\\ 1/\sqrt{2}&-1\sqrt{2}\end{pmatrix}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}1/\sqrt{50}&0\\ 0&1\sqrt{10}\end{pmatrix}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}0\\ -1/\sqrt{5}\end{pmatrix}$$

$$=\begin{pmatrix}1/\sqrt{2}&1/\sqrt{2}\\ 1/\sqrt{2}&-1\sqrt{2}\end{pmatrix}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}0\\ -1/\sqrt{50}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-1/10\\ 1/10\end{pmatrix}.$$

*Kjeldevarsel: i fasiten er fleire brøkar trykte utan brøkstrek — «$-1\sqrt{2}$» skal vere $-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ (i $P$ og i utrekninga av $x$) og «$1\sqrt{10}$» skal vere $\frac{1}{\sqrt{10}}$ (i $\Sigma^{-1}$); i leddet $Q^{H}\cdot b$ er $b$ skriven ein gong til mellom likskapsteikna, slik rekka skal lesast som at matrisa gonga med $b=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1&0&0\end{pmatrix}^{T}$ gir $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1/\sqrt{5}\end{pmatrix}^{T}$. Elles er alle matrisene kontrollreikna ($A^{H}A=\begin{pmatrix}30&20\\20&30\end{pmatrix}$, eigenverdiar $50$/$10$, $Q\Sigma P^{H}=A$, $A^{\dagger}=P\Sigma^{-1}Q^{H}$, $x=A^{\dagger}b=\begin{pmatrix}-1/10&1/10\end{pmatrix}^{T}$).*
