### **Problem 1**

Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1\\ -1&-1&1\end{pmatrix}$.

a) Find a singular value decomposition of $A$.

b) Find the pseudoinverse $A^{+}$ of $A$ and use it to find the best approximation to a solution of the system of linear equations:

$$x_1+x_2+x_3=1$$
$$x_1+x_2-x_3=1.$$
