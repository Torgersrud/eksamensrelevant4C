### **Problem 4**

Let the linear mapping $T:\mathbb{C}^{3}\to\mathbb{C}^{2}$ be given by the matrix

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}3&1&1\\ -1&3&1\end{pmatrix}$$

with respect to the standard bases. Compute the Singular Value Decomposition of $A$.
