### **Problem 2**

Find all the non-trivial solutions of the heat equation

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=2\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}\qquad\text{where}\qquad0\le x\le 2\pi\qquad\text{and}\qquad t\ge0\,,$$

that are of the form $u(x,t)=F(x)\cdot G(t)$, and that satisfy the boundary conditions

$$u(0,t)=0\qquad\text{and}\qquad\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(2\pi,t)=0\qquad\text{for every }t\ge0\,.$$

Use this to find a solution satisfying the initial condition

$$u(x,0)=\cos(x)\sin\left(\frac{x}{4}\right)\qquad\text{for every }0\le x\le 2\pi\,.$$
