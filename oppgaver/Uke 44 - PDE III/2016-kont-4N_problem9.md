### **Problem 9**

**a)** Given the equation

$$\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}(x,y)+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial y^2}(x,y)+5u(x,y)=0,\qquad 0<x<\pi,\quad 0<y<\pi/4,$$

find all solutions of the form $u(x,y)=F(x)G(y)$ that satisfy the boundary conditions

$$u(0,y)=0\qquad\text{and}\qquad u(\pi,y)=0,\qquad\qquad 0<y<\pi/4.$$

**b)** Find the solution of the problem in part **a)** that in addition satisfies the boundary conditions

$$\begin{array}{ll}u(x,0)=\sin(x),&0<x<\pi,\\ u(x,\pi/4)=\sin(x),&0<x<\pi.\end{array}$$
