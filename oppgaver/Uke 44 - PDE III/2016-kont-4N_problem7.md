### **Problem 7**

We want to find a numerical solution of the partial differential equation

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,t)=tu(x,t)+\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}(x,t),\qquad 0\le x\le 1,\qquad t>0,$$

with boundary conditions

$$u(0,t)=0,\qquad u(1,t)=1\qquad\text{for all }t>0$$

and initial condition

$$u(x,0)=x\qquad\text{for }0\le x\le 1.$$

Formulate an explicit method for solving this partial differential equation with the given boundary and initial conditions.

Use a step length of $h=1/4$ in space and perform two time steps of length $k=1/10$.
