### **Problem 5** [20 points]

Consider the heat equation

$$u_t=c^2u_{xx}+\alpha \tag{2}$$

where $c>0$ and $\alpha\in\mathbb{R}$ are given constants.

**a)** Show that the function

$$w(x,t)=\frac{\alpha}{2c^2}x(\pi-x)$$

satisfies the equation $w_t=c^2w_{xx}+\alpha$.

**b)** Find the solution of equation (2) for $x\in[0,\pi]$ and $t>0$ with the boundary conditions

$$u(0,t)=u(\pi,t)=0,\quad t>0,$$

and the initial condition

$$u(x,0)=\frac{\alpha}{2c^2}x(\pi-x)+\begin{cases}0,&0\leq x<\frac{\pi}{2},\\ x-\pi,&\frac{\pi}{2}<x\leq\pi.\end{cases}$$

**c)** Find $\lim_{t\to\infty}u(x,t)$.
