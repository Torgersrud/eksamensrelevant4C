### **Problem 4**

**a)** The steady state temperature of a plate of dimension $2\times 1$ (in metres) is modeled by the equation

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0,$$

for $0\leq x\leq 2$, $0\leq y\leq 1$. The right hand side of the plate is insulated, whilst the left hand side is held at a constant temperature of $0^{\circ}$C, i.e.

$$u(0,y)=u_x(2,y)=0,$$

Find all possible solutions $u(x,y)=F(x)G(y)$ of the equation satisfying the above conditions.

**b)** If the bottom edge of the plate is also insulated, i.e. $u_y(x,0)=0$, and the top edge is held at temperature

$$u(x,1)=(50\sin\frac{5\pi x}{4})^{\circ}\mathrm{C},$$

solve the equation to find the steady state temperature $u(x,y)$ of the plate.
