### **Problem 4**

Consider the following partial differential equation:

$$u_{t}-tu_{xx}=0,\quad x\in(0,1),\quad t>0,$$

$$u=0,\quad x=0\ \text{og}\ x=1.$$

**a)** Find all non-trivial solutions of the form $u(t,x)=G(t)F(x)$.

**b)** Determine the unique solution $u(t,x)$ satisfying the initial condition:

$$\left.u\right|_{t=0}=2\sin(\pi x)+\frac{5}{3}\sin(30\pi x)+\frac{1}{7}\sin(11\pi x).$$
