### **Problem 4**

Derive the solution formula

$$u(x,t)=\frac{1}{2c\sqrt{\pi t}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(v)e^{\frac{-(x-v)^2}{4c^2t}}\,dv,\quad t>0,$$

for the heat equation

$$u_t=c^2u_{xx},$$

on the whole $x$-axis with initial condition

$$u(x,0)=f(x).$$
