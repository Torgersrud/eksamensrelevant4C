### **Problem 3** Heat equation [12 pts]

Consider the following heat equation

$$u_t(x,t) = c^2 u_{xx}(x,t), \quad t \ge 0, x \in [0, \pi]$$

with boundary conditions

$$u(0,t) = u(\pi,t) = 0, \quad t \ge 0$$

and initial condition

$$u(x,0) = \frac{\pi}{2} - \left| x - \frac{\pi}{2} \right|, \quad x \in [0, \pi].$$

**a)** Show that the Fourier sine series solution of this above heat equation with boundary conditions is

$$u(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} u_n(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} B_n \sin(nx) e^{-c^2 n^2 t}$$

by using the separation of variables method.

**b)** Compute the Fourier sine series solution of the above heat equation with the given boundary conditions and initial condition. Write down the three first non-zero terms of the solution.
