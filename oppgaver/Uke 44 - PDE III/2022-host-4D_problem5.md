### **Problem 5.**

Use the Fourier transform in order to solve the PDE

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + u \qquad \text{for } x \in \mathbb{R} \text{ and } t > 0$$

with initial conditions

$$u(x, 0) = \begin{cases}\dfrac{\sin(x)}{x} & \text{if } x \neq 0,\\[1em] 1 & \text{if } x = 0.\end{cases}$$
