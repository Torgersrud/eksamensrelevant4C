### **Problem 4.**

Find all non-trivial solutions of the equation

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = 4 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} \qquad \text{where } -1 < x < 1 \qquad \text{and } t > 0$$

that are of the form $u(x, t) = F(x)G(t)$ and that satisfy the boundary conditions

$$u(-1, t) = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad u(1, t) = 0 \qquad \text{for } t > 0.$$
