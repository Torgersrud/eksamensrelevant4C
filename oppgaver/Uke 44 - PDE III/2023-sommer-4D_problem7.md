### **Problem 7.** (Separation of Variables)

Find all non-trivial solutions of the equation

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + u \qquad \text{where } 0 < x < \pi \qquad \text{and } t > 0$$

that are of the form $u(x, t) = F(x)G(t)$ and that satisfy the boundary conditions

$$u(0, t) = 0 \qquad \text{and} \qquad u(\pi, t) = 0 \qquad \text{for } t > 0.$$
