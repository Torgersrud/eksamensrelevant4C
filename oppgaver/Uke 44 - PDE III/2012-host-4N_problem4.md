### **Problem 4**

(Problem 3 for Math 4M)

Consider the partial differential equation

$$u_t=u_{xx}-5u\qquad(\ast)\qquad t>0,\ \ 0<x<\pi,$$

with boundary conditions

$$u_x(0,t)=0,\qquad u_x(\pi,t)=0.$$

**a)** Find all non-trivial solutions of $(\ast)$ of the product form $u(x,t)=F(x)G(t)$ that satisfy the boundary conditions.

**b)** Solve the initial-boundary value problem obtained by adding the initial condition

$$u(x,0)=\cos^{2}\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)-2\cos(5x).$$
