### **Problem 5** **Heat Equation [10 pts]**

Solve the problem

$$\left\{\begin{array}{ll}\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial t}u(t,x)=9\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2}u(t,x),&\text{for }0\le t,\;0\le x\le\pi,\\[2mm]\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial x}u(t,0)=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}u(\pi,0)=0\\[1mm]\qquad\text{correction: }=\displaystyle\frac{\partial}{\partial x}u(t,\pi)=0,&\text{for }0\le t,\\[1mm]\qquad u(0,x)=3\cos(x)-\cos(3x),&\text{for }0\le x\le\pi,\end{array}\right.$$

using separation of variables.
