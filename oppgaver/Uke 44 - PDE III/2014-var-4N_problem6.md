### **Problem 6**

In this assignment, we want to determine a numerical approximation to the solution of the following wave equation:

$$\begin{gathered}u_{tt}-2u_{xx}=\sin(t),\quad x\in(0,1),\ t>0,\\ u(t,0)=0,\quad t>0,\\ u(t,1)=0,\quad t>0,\\ \left.u\right|_{t=0}=0,\quad x\in(0,1),\\ \left.u_{t}\right|_{t=0}=1,\quad x\in(0,1).\end{gathered}\qquad(3)$$

Formulate a method that provides a numerical approximation to the solution $u$. What is the order of your method?
