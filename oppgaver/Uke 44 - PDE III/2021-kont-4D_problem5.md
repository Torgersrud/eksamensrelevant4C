### **Problem 5**

Consider the heat equation

$$u_t = c^2 u_{xx} + \alpha\cos(x - \pi) \qquad (2)$$

where $c > 0$ and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ are given constants.

a) Show that

$$v(x,t) = \frac{\alpha}{c^2}\cos(x - \pi)$$

is a solution of (2).

b) Find the solution of equation (2) for $x \in [0, \pi]$ and $t > 0$ such that

$$u_x(0,t) = u_x(\pi,t) = 0, \quad t > 0,$$

and

$$u(x,0) = \frac{\alpha}{c^2}\cos(x - \pi) + \begin{cases}0, & 0 \leq x < \frac{\pi}{2},\\ x - \pi, & \frac{\pi}{2} < x \leq \pi.\end{cases}$$

c) Find $\lim_{t \to \infty} u(x,t)$.
