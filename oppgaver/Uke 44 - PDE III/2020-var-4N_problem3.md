### **Problem 3** (Heat equation)

Given is the heat equation

$$\partial_t u(x,t) = \partial_{xx} u(x,t), \quad 0 \le x \le 4,$$

together with initial condition

$$u(x,0) = 2 \sin(3\pi x)$$

and boundary conditions

$$u(0,t) = u(4,t) = 0, \quad t > 0.$$

**a)** Find all solutions of the heat equation which are of the form $u(x,t) = F(x)G(t)$ and also satisfy the boundary conditions.

**b)** Find the solution $u$ to the heat equation which satisfies both the boundary and the initial conditions.
