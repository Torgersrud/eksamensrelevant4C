### **Problem 10** (B-Splines, 11 points)

We consider the cubic splines $\mathcal{S}_{3,\Delta}$ with knot vector $\Delta=[-1,0,1,2,3]$.

Which of the following functions is in the space $\mathcal{S}_{3,\Delta}$? State a reason for each of the functions.

a) $f_1(x)=3x^{3}$

b) $f_2(x)=\left|x-\frac{1}{2}\right|$

c) $f_3(x)=4(x)_{+}^{2}+2(x-2)_{+}^{3}$, where $(y)_{+}=\begin{cases}y&\text{if}\ y\geq 0\\ 0&\text{else}\end{cases}$

d) What is the dimension of the space $\mathcal{S}_{3,\Delta}$?

e) What does it mean for a spline $s(x)\in\mathcal{S}_{3,\Delta}$ to be *natural*?
