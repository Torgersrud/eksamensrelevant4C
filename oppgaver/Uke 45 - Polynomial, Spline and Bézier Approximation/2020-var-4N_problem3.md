### **Problem 3** (Compute interpolation polynomial, 8 pts)

Given the points $x_0 = 0.0$, $x_1 = 1.0$, $x_2 = 2.0$, $x_3 = 4.0$ together with 4 data points $f_0 = 1, f_1 = 5, f_2 = 10, f_3 = 12$. Find the polynomial of minimal degree interpolating through these points. Express it in the form

$$p_n(x) = a_n x^n + a_{n-1} x^{n-1} + \ldots + a_1 x + a_0.$$

Only state the final solution, do not give a detailed derivation.
