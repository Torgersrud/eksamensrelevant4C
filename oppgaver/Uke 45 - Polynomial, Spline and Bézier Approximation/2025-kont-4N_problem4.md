### **Problem 4** **Interpolation [10 pts]**

Consider the points
$$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}\hline x_i&0&1&2\\\hline y_i=f(x_i)&1&2&0\\\hline\end{array}$$
Compute the cubic polynomial $p(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$ that satisfies the above interpolation conditions and the additional derivative condition $p'(1)=3$.
