### **Problem 6**

**a)** Find the polynomial of lowest degree that interpolates the points

$$\begin{array}{c||c|c|c|c|c}x_i&0&\frac{1}{2}&1&\frac{3}{2}&2\\\hline f(x_i)&-11&-7&3&25&65\end{array}$$

**b)** The integral of $f$ over $[0,2]$ is approximated with Simpson's rule using the tabulated function values. What is the error?
