$$t(x)=c_0+c_1\cos x+c_2\sin x+c_3\cos 2x+c_4\sin 2x,$$

$$\begin{array}{c|ccccc}x_i&-\pi/2&-\pi/4&0&\pi/4&\pi/2\\\hline y_i&2&\sqrt{2}&0&\sqrt{2}&2.\end{array}$$
