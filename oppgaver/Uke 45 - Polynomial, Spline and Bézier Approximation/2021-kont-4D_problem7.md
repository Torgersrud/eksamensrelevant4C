### **Problem 7**

Let

$$f(x) = \begin{cases}\ln(x + 1) & (x > 0)\\ \sin(x) & (x \leq 0)\end{cases}$$

and consider the nodes $x_1 = -1$, $x_2 = 0$ and $x_3 = 1$.

a) What is the degree of the polynomial interpolation using the above nodes?

b) Compute the polynomial interpolation to $f$ using the Lagrange form.

c) Determine the Newton form of the interpolating polynomial.
