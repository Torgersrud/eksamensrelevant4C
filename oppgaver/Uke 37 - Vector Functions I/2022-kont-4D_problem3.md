### **Problem 3.** (Partial derivatives, 10 points)

Consider the function $f : \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}$

$$f(x, y, z) = x^2 \sin(y) e^{-z} - 2x^2$$

For this function, compute each of the following partial derivatives:

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}, \qquad \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \frac{\partial^3 f}{\partial x\, \partial y\, \partial z}.$$
