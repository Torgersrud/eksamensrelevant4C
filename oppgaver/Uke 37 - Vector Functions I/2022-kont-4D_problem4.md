### **Problem 4.** (Nonlinear Equations (Fixed Point Theory), 12 pts)

Let the sequence $(x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0}$ be defined by

$$x_{n+1} := \frac{3x_n + 1}{2x_n + 1}$$

with the starting value $x_0 := 1$.

a) Check whether the sequence $(x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}_0}$ is convergent and, if so, compute the limit value $\widehat{x} := \lim\limits_{n \to \infty} x_n$.

b) Determine an upper bound for $|x_5 - \widehat{x}|$ without calculating $x_5$.
