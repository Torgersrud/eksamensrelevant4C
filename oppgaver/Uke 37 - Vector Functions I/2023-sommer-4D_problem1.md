### **Problem 1.** (Fixed-point iterations)

Consider the nonlinear equation $x - \sqrt{\sin x} = 0$, with $x \in [\pi/6, \pi/2]$, for which we can write the fixed-point iteration as

$$x_{k+1} = \sqrt{\sin x_k} \, .$$

a) Show that the nonlinear equation has a unique solution $r$ in the interval $[\pi/6, \pi/2]$, and that the fixed-point iterations above will converge to $r$ for any initial guess $x_0$ in that interval.

b) Starting from $x_0 = \pi/2$, find an upper bound for the error $|x_{k+1} - r|$ after $k = 60$ iterations.
**Important:** you are *not* being asked to perform these iterations!
