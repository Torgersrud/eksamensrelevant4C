### **Solution to Problem 7**

Put

$$f(x)=x+\ln(x-1),$$

then

$$f'(x)=1+\frac{1}{x-1}>1$$

when $x>1$. But $f(1+)=-\infty<0$, $f(2)=2>0$. Thus we have exact one solution inside $(1,2)$. By Newton's method

$$x_1=x_0-\frac{f(x_0)}{f'(x_0)}=\frac{5+\ln 4}{5}\approx 1.27725887222.$$
