### **Løsning til Oppgave 6**

La $u = x^2 + y^2$. Kjerneregelen gir

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{dz}{du}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = 2x\frac{df}{du} \qquad \text{og} \qquad \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = \frac{dz}{du}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = 2y\frac{df}{du},$$

slik at

$$y\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - x\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 2xy\frac{df}{du} - 2xy\frac{df}{du} = 0$$

som var det vi skulle vise.
