### **Løsning til Oppgave 3**

Ved kjerneregelen (kjederegelen) gjelder

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{\partial T}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial T}{\partial y} \cdot \frac{dy}{dt} + \frac{\partial T}{\partial z} \cdot \frac{dz}{dt} = \left\langle \frac{\partial T}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial T}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial T}{\partial z} \right\rangle \cdot \left\langle \frac{dx}{dt}, \frac{dy}{dt}, \frac{dz}{dt} \right\rangle$$

$$= \langle 2, 1, -4 \rangle \cdot \frac{\langle 7, 5, 1 \rangle}{|\langle 7, 5, 1 \rangle|} \cdot 1000 \ {}^\circ\text{C/h} = \frac{14 + 5 - 4}{\sqrt{49 + 25 + 1}} \cdot \frac{1000}{60} \ {}^\circ\text{C/min} = \frac{50}{\sqrt{3}} \ {}^\circ\text{C/min}.$$
