### **Problem 1**

Let $f:[0,2]\to\mathbb{R}$ be given by

$$f(x)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll} 0 & \mathrm{for}\ 0<x\leq 1\\ 1-x & \mathrm{for}\ 1<x\leq 2 \end{array}\right.$$

Let $g:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be the odd 4-periodic extension of $f$. Sketch its graph on the interval $[-6,6]$ and compute the Fourier series of $g$.
