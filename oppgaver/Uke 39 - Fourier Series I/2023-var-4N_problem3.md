### **Problem 3** **Fourier Series (14 points)**

a) Let the function $g(x)=-x$, $x\in[0,\pi]$, be given and consider the $2\pi$-periodic function $f_3$ obtained from the even extension $g_e$ of $g$ defined on $[-\pi,\pi]$ by periodisation.
Sketch the function $f_3$ on at least 3 intervals.

b) Compute all coefficients of the real Fourier series of $f_3$ from a).

c) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function $f_1(x)=7e^{-ix}+5e^{3ix}+3e^{-5ix}+e^{7ix}$, $x\in[-\pi,\pi)$.
Compute all coefficients of the complex Fourier series of $f_1$.

d) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function $f_2(x)=2(\cos(2x))^2+2\sin(4x)-2\cos(8x)$, $x\in[-\pi,\pi)$.
Compute all coefficients of the complex Fourier series of $f_2$.
