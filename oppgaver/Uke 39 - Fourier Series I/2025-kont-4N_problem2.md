### **Problem 2** **Fourier series [10 pts]**

Extend the function
$$f(x)=-x+1,$$
defined on the interval $[0,1)$, to a periodic function in such a way that the extension has period 2 and is odd. Draw a plot of this new function on the interval $[-1,1)$. Find the Fourier series of this new function, and explicitly write down the first 5 nonvanishing terms of the Fourier series.
