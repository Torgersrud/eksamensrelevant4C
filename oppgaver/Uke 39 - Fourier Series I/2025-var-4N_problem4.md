### **Problem 4** **Fourier Series (12 points)**

a) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function
$$f(x)=2\cos(4x)+3e^{6ix}+8e^{9ix}+5e^{-7ix}.$$
Find all coefficients $c_k(f)$, $k\in\mathbb{Z}$, of the complex Fourier series of $f$.

b) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function
$$g(x)=2\bigl(\cos(2x)\bigr)^2+\sin(5x)+2\cos(5x)+\sin(9x).$$
Find all coefficients $a_0(g)$, $a_n(g)$, $b_n(g)$, $n\in\mathbb{N}$ of the real Fourier series of $g$

c) Let $h=f*g$ denote the convolution of the two functions from Item a) and Item b). Compute all coefficients of the real as well as the complex Fourier series of $h$. Use these to find the function $h$.
