### **Problem 1**

Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic functions defined by $f(x)=\cos\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)$ when $x\in[-\pi,\pi]$. Make a drawing of the function $f$ for the interval $[-3\pi,3\pi]$, and compute the Fourier series of $f$. Use the result to compute the value of the series

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n}{1-4n^2}\,.$$
