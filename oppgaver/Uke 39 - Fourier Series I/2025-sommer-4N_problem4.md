### **Problem 4** **Fourier Series (12 points)**

a) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function
$$f(x)=9\sin(3x)+3e^{-5ix}+2e^{7ix}+e^{-9ix}.$$
Find all coefficients $c_k(f)$, $k\in\mathbb{Z}$, of the complex Fourier series of $f$.

b) We further consider the so-called *Dirichlet kernel*
$$D_N(x)=\sum_{k=-N}^{N}e^{ikx},\qquad\text{for some }N\in\mathbb{N}.$$
Find all coefficients $c_k(D_N)$ of the complex Fourier series of $D_N$. Keep in mind that these might depend on $N$.

c) For any $N\in\mathbb{N}$, let $g_N=f*D_N$ denote the convolution of the functions $f$ from Item a) and the Dirichlet Kernel $D_N$ from Item b).
Compute the two functions $g_5$ and $g_{10}$ and interpret the result by comparing their Fourier coefficients.

d) Often one uses the so-called *modified Dirichlet-Kernel* $\tilde D_N$ defined as $\tilde D_N(x)=\frac{1}{2\pi}D_N(x)$.
State two reasons, why the modified Dirichlet kernel is preferred over the original Dirichlet kernel $D_N$.
