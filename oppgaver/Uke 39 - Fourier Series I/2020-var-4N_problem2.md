### **Problem 2** (Fourier series)

Again, let $u$ be the Heaviside function.

**a)** Sketch the function $-u(x+\pi) + 2u(x)$ over the interval $[-\pi, \pi]$.

**b)** Compute the Fourier series $f \sim a_0 + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n \cos nx + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n \sin nx$ for

the function $f(x) = \cos 3x - \dfrac{1}{2}\sin 2x - u(x+\pi) + 2u(x)$.
