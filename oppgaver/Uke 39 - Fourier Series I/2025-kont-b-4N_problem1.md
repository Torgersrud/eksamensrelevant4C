### **Problem 1** **Fourier Series (10 points)**

Consider the periodic function $f(t)$ with period $2\pi$, defined by:
$$f(t)=\begin{cases}1,&0<t<\pi,\\ -1,&-\pi<t<0,\\ 0,&t=0,\pm\pi,\end{cases}$$
and extended periodically outside $-\pi<t<\pi$.

a) Determine whether $f(t)$ is even, odd, or neither. Explain your reasoning and what this means for the Fourier series of $f(t)$.

b) Compute the Fourier series of $f(t)$, giving explicit expressions for the Fourier coefficients $a_0$, $a_n$, $b_n$.

c) Write the first three nonzero terms of the Fourier series explicitly.
