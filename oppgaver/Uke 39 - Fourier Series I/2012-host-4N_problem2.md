### **Problem 2**

Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic function given on $(-\pi,\pi)$ by

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}0,&-\pi<x<0,\\ x,&0<x<\pi.\end{cases}$$

**a)** Sketch the graph of $f$ on the interval $[-3\pi,3\pi)$ and determine the Fourier series of $f$.

**b)** Use the Fourier series to show that

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(2k+1)^{2}}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}.$$
