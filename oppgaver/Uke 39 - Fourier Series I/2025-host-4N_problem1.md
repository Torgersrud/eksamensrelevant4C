### **Problem 1** **Fourier Series (10 points)**

Consider the periodic function $f(t)$ with period $2\pi$, defined by

$$f(t)=\begin{cases}t,&-\pi<t<0,\\ \pi-t,&0<t<\pi,\\ 0,&t=0,\pm\pi,\end{cases}$$

and extended periodically outside $-\pi<t<\pi$.

a) Determine whether $f(t)$ is even, odd, or neither. Explain your reasoning and discuss what this implies for the Fourier series of $f(t)$.

b) Compute the Fourier series of $f(t)$, giving explicit expressions for the Fourier coefficients $a_0,a_n,b_n$. Simplify as much as possible.

c) Write the first three nonzero terms of the Fourier series explicitly.
