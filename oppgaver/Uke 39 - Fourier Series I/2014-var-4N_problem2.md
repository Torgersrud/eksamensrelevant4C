### **Problem 2**

The following function

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}\frac{2x}{\pi},&0\le x<\frac{\pi}{2},\\[4pt] \frac{2}{\pi}(\pi-x),&\frac{\pi}{2}\le x\le\pi,\end{cases}\qquad(1)$$

is to be extended to an $odd$ function $g$ defined on $\mathbb{R}$ with period $2\pi$.

**a)** Sketch the graph of $g$ on the interval $[-3\pi,3\pi]$ and show that $g$ may be written

$$g(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n}8}{\pi^{2}(2n+1)^{2}}\sin\left((2n+1)x\right).$$

**b)** Using the Fourier series of $g$, prove that

$$1+\frac{1}{3^{2}}+\frac{1}{5^{2}}+\frac{1}{7^{2}}+\frac{1}{9^{2}}+\frac{1}{11^{2}}+\cdots=\frac{\pi^{2}}{8}.$$

**c)** An approximation of $f$ using $N$ Fourier components is given by

$$S^{N}(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\frac{(-1)^{n}8}{\pi^{2}(2n+1)^{2}}\sin\left((2n+1)x\right).$$

Prove that the $L^{2}$-error:

$$E=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_{0}^{\pi}\left|S^{N}(x)-f(x)\right|^{2}\ dx,$$

satisfies the bound

$$E<\frac{8}{\pi^{2}(2N+1)^{2}}.$$
