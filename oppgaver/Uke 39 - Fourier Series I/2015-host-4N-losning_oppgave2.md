### **Løsning til Oppgave 2**

We use Parseval's identity, which states that

$$\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x)^2\,dx=2a_0^2+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(a_n^2+b_n^2\right).$$

In this case, this means that

$$2\frac{\pi^4}{9}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{16}{n^4}=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}x^4\,dx=\frac{2\pi^4}{5}.$$

Thus

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^4}=\frac{1}{16}\left(\frac{2\pi^4}{5}-\frac{2\pi^4}{9}\right)=\frac{\pi^4}{90}.$$
