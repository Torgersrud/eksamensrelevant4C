### **Problem 6.** (4D only)

Classify the PDE

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2} + u \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$$

as linear or nonlinear.

Show that, for given constants $a$ and $b$,

$$u(x, t) = a + \frac{2}{at + x + b}$$

is a solution of the PDE.
