### **Problem 5** **D'Alembert (6 points)**

Find the solution of the following PDE on the real line
$$\begin{cases}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2}=9\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}&\text{where }x\in\mathbb{R}\text{ and }t>0\\ u(x,0)=\frac{2}{1+x^2},&x\in\mathbb{R},\\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,0)=\frac{12x}{(1+x^2)^2},&x\in\mathbb{R},\end{cases}$$
using d'Alembert's method.
