### **Solution to Problem 4**

Notice that outside the origin we have

$$u_x = 2x/(x^2 + y^2)$$

which gives

$$u_{xx} = 2/(x^2 + y^2) - 4x^2/(x^2 + y^2)^2 = 2(y^2 - x^2)/(x^2 + y^2)^2.$$

Similarly

$$u_y = 2y/(x^2 + y^2)$$

and

$$u_{yy} = 2(x^2 - y^2)/(x^2 + y^2)^2,$$

hence

$$u_{xx} + u_{yy} = 0.$$
