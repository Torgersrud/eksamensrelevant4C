### **Løsning til Oppgave 3**

If

$$h(x,t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi t}}\,e^{-\frac{x^2}{2t}},$$

then

$$h_t=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\cdot\frac{-1}{2}\cdot t^{\frac{-3}{2}}\cdot e^{-\frac{x^2}{2t}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi t}}\,e^{-\frac{x^2}{2t}}\cdot\frac{-x^2}{2}\cdot\frac{-1}{t^2}=\frac{x^2-t}{2t^2}h,$$

and

$$h_x=-\frac{x}{t}h,\;\;h_{xx}=-\frac{1}{t}h+\frac{x^2}{t^2}h=\frac{x^2-t}{t^2}h.$$

Thus we get that

$$h_t=\frac{1}{2}h_{xx}.$$
