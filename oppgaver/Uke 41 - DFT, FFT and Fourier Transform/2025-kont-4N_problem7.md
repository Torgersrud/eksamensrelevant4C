### **Problem 7** **Fourier Transform [10 pts]**

a) Show for $a\neq0$ that
$$F(f(at))(\omega)=\frac{1}{|a|}F(f(t))\left(\frac{\omega}{a}\right).$$

b) Consider the function
$$\tilde{h}(\omega)=\frac{\exp(-\omega^2)}{1+\omega^2}.$$
Write the given $\tilde{h}$ as a product of two familiar Fourier transforms $\tilde{f}$ and $\tilde{g}$. Identify the functions $f$ and $g$ and express the function $h$ as a convolution.
