### **Problem 3**

Let a function $f$ on $(-\infty,\infty)$ is defined as

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}\cos x, & \text{if } |x|<1;\\ 0, & \text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$

Find the Fourier transform of $f$ and then evaluate the integral

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin 2w}{w}\,\cos w\ \,dw.$$
