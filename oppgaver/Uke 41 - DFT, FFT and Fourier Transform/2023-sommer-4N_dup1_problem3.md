### **Problem 3.** (Discrete Fourier Transform)

In this task we consider the discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) for signals of length $n = 8$. We denote by $x_j = 2\pi j/8$, $j = 0, \ldots, 7$, corresponding sampling points on $[0, 2\pi)$.

a) We consider the function $f(x) = e^{3\mathrm{i}x}$, $x \in [0, 2\pi)$, and its sampling values $f_j = f(x_j)$ at the points $x_j$ from above.
Compute the discrete Fourier transform $\hat{\mathbf{f}}$ of the vector $\mathbf{f} = (f_0, \ldots, f_7)$.

b) Let $\hat{\mathbf{g}} = (0, \mathrm{i}, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, -\mathrm{i})$ be a result of a DFT. Is the original signal $\mathbf{g} = \mathcal{F}_8^{-1} \hat{\mathbf{g}}$ real-valued?
