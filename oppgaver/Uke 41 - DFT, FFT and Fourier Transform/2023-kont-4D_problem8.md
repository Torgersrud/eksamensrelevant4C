### **Problem 8** **Discrete Fourier Transform [10 pts]**

Let $c=(c_0,c_1,...,c_{N-1})$ be the discrete Fourier transform of the signal $f=(f_0,f_1,...,f_{N-1})$. We use the notation $w=e^{2\pi i/N}$. Let $\tilde f=(f_1,...,f_{N-1},f_0)$ be the cyclic shift of the initial signal $f$. Prove that the discrete Fourier transform of $\tilde f$ is given by $\tilde c=(\tilde c_1,...,\tilde c_N)$ with $\tilde c_j=w^jc_j$.
