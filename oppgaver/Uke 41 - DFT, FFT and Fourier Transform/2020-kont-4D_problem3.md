### **Problem 3** [10 points]

Define the convolution for functions $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ and $g:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ as

$$(f*g)(x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}}f(x-y)g(y)\,dy.$$

Use the convolution theorem to compute $f*f$, where

$$f(x)=e^{-x^2/(2a)},$$

and $a>0$ is a constant.
