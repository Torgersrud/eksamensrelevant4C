### **Problem 3** **Fourier Series (10 points)**

a) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function
$$g(x)=2e^{-ix}+3e^{5ix}+5e^{-9ix}+7e^{16ix}.$$
Find all coefficients $c_k(g)$, $k\in\mathbb{Z}$, of the complex Fourier series of $g$.

b) Consider the $2\pi$-periodic function
$$h(x)=2\bigl(\sin(3x)\bigr)^2+\sin(6x)-2\sin(9x).$$
Find all coefficients $c_k(h)$, $k\in\mathbb{Z}$, of the complex Fourier series of $h$ as well as all coefficients $a_n(h)$, $b_n(h)$ of the real Fourier series of $h$.

c) We consider the discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) with $N$ samples $f_j=f(x_j)$, where $x_j=\frac{2\pi j}{N}$, $j=0,\dots,N-1$, of a $2\pi$-periodic function $f$.
How is the DFT computed most efficiently and what is then the computational effort?

d) Let $g_j=g(x_j)$, $j=0,\dots,N-1$, denote the sampling values of $g$ similar to c) and $\hat g_k$, $k=0,\dots,N-1$, the resulting discrete Fourier coefficients.
What is the relation of the discrete Fourier coefficients $\hat g_k$ to the Fourier coefficients $c_k(g)$?
Use this to compute the $\hat g_k$, $k=0,\dots,N-1$ for $N=8$.
