### **Oppgave 2**

La

$$f(x)=\frac{4}{x^2+2}\qquad\text{og}\qquad g(x)=\frac{2x}{(x^2+1)^2},\qquad \text{for alle reelle } x.$$

Vis at foldingen (convolution) er

$$(f*g)(x)=-\pi\sqrt{2}i\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\omega e^{-(\sqrt{2}+1)|\omega|}e^{i\omega x}d\omega.$$
