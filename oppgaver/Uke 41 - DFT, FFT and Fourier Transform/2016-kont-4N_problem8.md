### **Problem 8**

Use the Fourier transformation for solving the partial differential equation

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,t)=t\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}(x,t),\qquad x\in\mathbb{R},\qquad t>0,$$

with initial condition

$$u(x,0)=e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}},\qquad\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}.$$
