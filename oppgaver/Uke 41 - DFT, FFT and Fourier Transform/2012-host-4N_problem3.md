### **Problem 3**

(Problem 2 for Math 4M)

Let $f(x)=x\,e^{-a|x|}$ for $x\in\mathbb{R}$, where $a>0$ is a constant.

**a)** Compute the Fourier transform

$$\hat{f}(w)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,e^{-iwx}\,dx.$$

**b)** Use the Fourier-inversion formula to evaluate the integral

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{w\sin(w)}{(1+w^{2})^{2}}\,dw.$$
