### **Solution to Problem 3**

The Fourier transform of the Gaussian can be found in a table:

$$\hat{f}(\xi)=\sqrt{a}e^{-\xi^{2}a/2}.$$

By the convolution theorem for Fourier transforms,

$$\widehat{f*f}=\hat{f}^{2}.$$

Therefore

$$\widehat{f*f}(\xi)=ae^{-\xi^{2}a}.$$

Again from the formula sheet and the uniqueness of the Fourier transform,

$$(f*f)(x)=\sqrt{\frac{a}{2}}e^{-x^{2}/(4a)}.$$
