### **Problem 4** *TMA4130 Mathematics 4N:* [5 points]

Show that the Fourier transform of

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}x^{2},&|x|<1,\\ 0,&|x|\ge 1,\end{cases}$$

is the function

$$\hat{f}(w)=\begin{cases}\frac{1}{3}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}},&&w=0,\\ \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\left(\frac{\sin w}{w}+\frac{2\cos w}{w^{2}}-\frac{2\sin w}{w^{3}}\right),&&w\ne 0,\end{cases}$$
