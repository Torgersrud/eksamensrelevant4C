### **Problem 3.**

The function $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ given by

$$f(x) = 2 + x \qquad \text{for } 0 \leq x \leq 1$$

is to be extended to an odd function $g$ with period 2. Sketch the graph of $g$ on the interval $[-3, 3]$ and compute the Fourier series of $g$.
