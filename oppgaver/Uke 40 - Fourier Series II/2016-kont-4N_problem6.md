### **Problem 6**

Let $f$ be the $2\pi$-periodic function given by

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}e^x&\text{for }0<x<\pi,\\ 0&\text{for }-\pi<x<0.\end{cases}$$

Assume that $a_n$ and $b_n$ are the Fourier coefficients of $f$, and denote by $g$ and $h$ the functions

$$g(x)=a_0+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n\cos(nx)\qquad og\qquad h(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}b_n\sin(nx).$$

Sketch the graphs of the functions $f$, $g$, and $h$ on the interval $[-2\pi,2\pi]$, and find the values of $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ in the points $x=-\pi/2$, $x=0$, and $x=\pi/2$.
