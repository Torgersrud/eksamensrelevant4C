### **Problem 2** **Fourier series [10 pts]**

Extend the function

$$f(x)=2x,$$

defined on the interval $[0,2)$, to the interval $[0,4)$ in such a way that the periodic extension of this function is **even** with a period of 4. Find the Fourier series of this new function, and explicitly write down the first 4 nonvanishing terms of the Fourier series.
