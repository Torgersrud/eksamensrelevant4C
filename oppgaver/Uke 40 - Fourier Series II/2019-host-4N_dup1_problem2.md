### **Problem 2** [14 points]

**a)** Let

$$f(x)=1+x,\quad -\pi<x<\pi.$$

Verify the following complex Fourier series expansion for $f$

$$1+\sum_{n\neq 0}\frac{i(-1)^n}{n}e^{inx}.$$

**b)** Why is

$$f(x)=1+\sum_{n\neq 0}\frac{i(-1)^n}{n}e^{inx}$$

for $-\pi<x<\pi$?

**c)** Compute the Fourier transform of

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}\sin(x)&|x|<1,\\ 0&|x|\geq 1.\end{cases}$$
