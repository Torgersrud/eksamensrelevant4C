### **Problem 4**

*(Inner products, least distances)*

**b)** In $L^{2}((-\pi,\pi),\mathbb{C})$ with inner product $\langle x,y\rangle=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}x(t)\overline{y(t)}\,dt$, find the minimal distance between $M=\text{span}\{1,\mathrm{e}^{it},\mathrm{e}^{2it}\}$ and the function $x_0$ given by $x_0(t)=t$.
