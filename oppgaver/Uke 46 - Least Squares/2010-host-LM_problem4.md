### **Problem 4**

Define $W\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{4}$ as the subspace:

$$W:=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}w\\ x\\ y\\ z\end{bmatrix}\ \text{ with }\begin{aligned} -4w+4x+3y+5z&=0,\\ -6w+6x+3y+9z&=0,\\ -2w+2x\phantom{+3y}+4z&=0,\\ 2w-2x-3y-z&=0\end{aligned}\right\}$$

Find the closest point in $W$ to the vector

$$\begin{bmatrix}7\\ -5\\ -1\\ -3\end{bmatrix}.$$
