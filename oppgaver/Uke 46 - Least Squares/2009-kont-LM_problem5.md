### **Problem 5**

Let $C([0,1],\mathbb{C})$ denote the space of continuous functions from $[0,1]$ to $\mathbb{C}$. Let

$$(f,g)=\int_{0}^{1}f(t)\overline{g(t)}dt$$

be the standard inner product on $C([0,1],\mathbb{C})$.

**c.** Find $a,b,c$ such that

$$\int_{0}^{1}\left\lvert 4t^{2}-4t-a\sin(2\pi t)-b\cos(\pi t)-c\right\rvert^{2}dt$$

is minimal. (3 points)

*Kjeldevarsel: oppgåvens punkt a) (at $R:f(t)\mapsto f(1-t)$ er selvadjungert) og b) (dekomposisjon $f=f_{+}+f_{-}$ i $R$-eigenrom) er operator-/indreproduktteori og er utelatte her.*
