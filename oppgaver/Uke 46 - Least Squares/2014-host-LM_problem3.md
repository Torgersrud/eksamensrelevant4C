### **Problem 3**

**a)** Let $C([0,2]\times[0,2],\mathbb{R})$ be an inner-product space with

$$\langle f,g\rangle=\int_0^2\int_0^2 f(x,y)g(x,y)dxdy.$$

Find an orthogonal basis for $\text{span}\{1,x,y\}$ in this space.

**b)** Find $a,b,c\in\mathbb{R}$ such that $\ \int_0^2\int_0^2\lvert xy-a-bx-cy\rvert^{2}dxdy\ $ is minimal.
