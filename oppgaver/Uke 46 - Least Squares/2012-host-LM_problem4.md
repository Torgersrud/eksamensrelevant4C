### **Problem 4**

*(Normed spaces, minimal distances)*

Let $M\subset X$ be a subset of normed space $(X,\lVert\cdot\rVert)$, $x_0\in X$. In each of the cases listed, calculate $\inf_{y\in M}\lVert x_0-y\rVert$ and determine whether there exists a unique $y_0\in M$ such that $\lVert x_0-y_0\rVert=\inf_{y\in M}\lVert x_0-y\rVert$. If so, find $y_0$.

**a)** $X=\mathbb{R}^{3}$ with norm $\lVert x\rVert=(\sum_{j=1}^{3}x_j^{2})^{1/2}$, $M=\text{span}\{(1,1,0)\}$, $x_0=(1,2,3)$.

**c)** $X=L_2((0,1),\mathbb{R})$ with norm $\lVert x\rVert=(\int_0^{1}|x(t)|^{2}dt)^{1/2}$, $M=\text{span}\{1,t\}$, the function $x_0$ given by $x_0(t)=t^{2}$.

*Kjeldevarsel: oppgåvens punkt b) — same spørsmål i $l_\infty$ med $\sup$-norm — handlar om rom utan indre produkt og er utelatt her; løysingsforslaget reknar likevel punktet ut, det er utelatt i løysningsfila også.*
