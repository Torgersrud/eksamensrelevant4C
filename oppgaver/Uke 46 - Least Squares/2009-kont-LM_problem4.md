### **Problem 4**

In this problem we equip $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ and $\mathbb{R}^{4}$ with their standard Euclidean norms:

$$\lVert x\rVert=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}}\qquad(n=3\text{ or }4)$$

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}5&2&1\\ -1&-1&1\\ 3&2&-1\\ -3&-1&-1\end{bmatrix},\quad b=\begin{bmatrix}-3\\ -3\\ 3\\ 3\end{bmatrix},\quad c=\begin{bmatrix}0\\ 3\\ 12\\ -9\end{bmatrix}$$

**a.** Find an $x\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ such that $Ax=b$. (3 points)

**b.** Find $\ker A$, and hence find the point $y\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ with the smallest norm such that $Ay=b$. (2 points)

**c.** Find a $z\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ such that $Az$ is the nearest point to $c$. (3 points)
