### **Problem 4**

Let:

$$W:=\left\{v\begin{bmatrix}2\\ 4\\ -1\\ -1\\ 0\end{bmatrix}+w\begin{bmatrix}-3\\ -3\\ 0\\ 2\\ 0\end{bmatrix}+x\begin{bmatrix}-3\\ -3\\ 1\\ 1\\ 1\end{bmatrix}+y\begin{bmatrix}3\\ 3\\ -1\\ -1\\ 0\end{bmatrix}+z\begin{bmatrix}2\\ 4\\ 0\\ -2\\ 0\end{bmatrix}:\begin{aligned} v-w-x+y+z&=0\\ x-2y-2z&=0\\ 2v-2w-x\phantom{+y+z}&=0\end{aligned}\right\},\ \text{and }b=\begin{bmatrix}2\\ 0\\ 0\\ 0\\ 0\end{bmatrix}.$$

Find the closest point to $b$ in $W^{\perp}$.
