### **Problem 4**

For $k\in\mathbb{N}$, let $\mathrm{Poly}_{k}$ be the vector space of polynomials with complex coefficients of degree at most $k$. Define a function $\mathrm{Poly}_{3}\times\mathrm{Poly}_{3}\to\mathbb{C}$ by

$$\langle p,q\rangle=\sum_{j=0}^{3}p(j)\overline{q(j)}$$

**1.** Prove that this is an inner product on $\mathrm{Poly}_{3}$. (3 points)

**2.** Apply the Gram–Schmidt algorithm to the family $\{1,t\}$ to find an orthonormal basis for $\mathrm{Poly}_{1}$, regarded as a subspace of $\mathrm{Poly}_{3}$ in the obvious way. (2 points)

**3.** Let $p\in\mathrm{Poly}_{3}$ be a polynomial such that $p(0)=-3$, $p(1)=1$, $p(2)=3$, and $p(3)=3$. Find the orthogonal projection of $p$ on to $\mathrm{Poly}_{1}$ (that is, find the closest point to $p$ in the subspace $\mathrm{Poly}_{1}$). (2 points)

**4.** Is $\deg p\le 1$? (1 point)
