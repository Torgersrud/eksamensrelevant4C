### **Problem 2**

In this problem we equip $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ and $\mathbb{R}^{4}$ with their standard Euclidean norms:

$$\lVert x\rVert=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+\cdots+x_{n}^{2}}\qquad(n=3\text{ or }4)$$

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&3&4\\ 2&2&4\\ 3&-1&2\\ 4&-1&3\end{bmatrix},\quad b=\begin{bmatrix}2\\ 0\\ -4\\ -5\end{bmatrix},\quad c=\begin{bmatrix}0\\ 3\\ 0\\ -9\end{bmatrix}$$

**a.** Find an $x\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ such that $Ax=b$.

**b.** Find the set of $y\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ such that $Ay$ is the nearest point to $c$.

**c.** Find the point $z\in\mathbb{R}^{3}$ with the smallest norm such that $Az$ is the nearest point to $c$.
