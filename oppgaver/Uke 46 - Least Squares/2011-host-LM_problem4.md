### **Problem 4**

Let $\text{Poly}_3$ be the space of polynomials with real coefficients of degree at most 3 with the inner product:

$$\langle p,q\rangle=\int_{-1}^{1}p(t)q(t)dt.$$

Let $W\subseteq\text{Poly}_3$ be the subset of those polynomials $p(t)$ which satisfy all of the following conditions:

$$3\int_{-1}^{1}(t^{2}+t)p(t)dt=\int_{-1}^{1}(1+5t^{3})p(t)dt$$

$$\int_{-1}^{1}(-6t^{2}+5t^{3})p(t)dt=\int_{-1}^{1}(-2+3t)p(t)dt$$

$$\int_{-1}^{1}(-3+6t)p(t)dt=\int_{-1}^{1}(-9t^{2}+10t^{3})p(t)dt$$

Find the closest point in $W$ to $p(t)=3t^{2}-5t^{3}$.
