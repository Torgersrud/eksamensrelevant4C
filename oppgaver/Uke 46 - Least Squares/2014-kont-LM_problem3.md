### **Problem 3**

Let

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0\\ -1&2&-2\\ 1&-2&1\\ -1&-1&1\end{bmatrix}$$

1. Compute the $QR$-decomposition of $A$.

2. Let $W=\text{Im }A\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{4}$. Find the matrix of the orthogonal projection $\mathbb{R}^{4}\to W$.

3. Let $\vec{c}=\begin{bmatrix}1\\ 2\\ 2\\ -1\end{bmatrix}$. Find the nearest point in $W$ to $\vec{c}$.
