### **Problem 3**

Find $a,b\in\mathbb{C}$ such that

$$\int_{0}^{2\pi}\lvert x(t)-a\cos t-b\sin t\rvert^{2}dt$$

is minimal when $x$ is given by

$$x(t)=\begin{cases}1&0\le t\le\alpha\\ 0&\alpha<t\le 2\pi\end{cases}$$

where $\alpha\in(0,2\pi)$ is a constant.
