### **Problem 5**

*(Inner products, Hilbert spaces)*

**a)** In $\mathbb{R}^{4}$, let

$$M=\text{span}\{(1,2,3,2),(1,0,1,1)\}\quad\text{ and }\quad y=(2,0,3,1).$$

Calculate $d=\text{dist}(y,M)$. Is there a point $x_0\in M$ with $\lVert x_0-y\rVert=d$ (if so, explain why and determine it; if not, justify why there cannot be one)?
