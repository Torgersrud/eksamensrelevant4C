### **Problem 11** (What the formats cost: CP and Tucker storage against the raw tensor, 8 points)

a) A hyperspectral camera records a cube with $320$ spatial columns, $256$ spatial rows and $120$ spectral bands: a tensor $\mathbf{X}\in\mathbb{R}^{320\times256\times120}$. How many entries is that, and how many bytes does the cube occupy as `float64`? Compare the storage of a rank-$6$ CP representation with that of a Tucker representation with core $6\times6\times6$ (give both counts and each ratio against the raw cube). The Tucker format stores $216$ numbers more than the CP format — what do those numbers buy in one sentence? (2 P.)

b) A simulation logs a $4$-way tensor of size $100\times80\times60\times40$ compressed by a Tucker format with core $3\times3\times3\times3$. State the general storage formula for a $d$-way tensor $n_1\times\cdots\times n_d$ with core $r_1\times\cdots\times r_d$ (core plus factors), and use it to count the numbers stored here. How many does a rank-$3$ CP representation store, which format is smaller, and by how much? Give each ratio against the $19\,200\,000$ raw entries. (2 P.)

c) On a $50\times40\times30$ tensor a rank-$2$ CP format stores $2(50+40+30)=240$ numbers and a Tucker format with core $(2,2,2)$ stores $248$, against $60\,000$ raw entries. Every rank-$2$ CP is such a Tucker tensor — which $6$ of the $8$ core entries must be zero for a core $(2,2,2)$ to *be* a rank-$2$ CP? Show that the inclusion is **strict** by studying the tensor $\mathbf{W}\in\mathbb{R}^{2\times2\times2}$ with frontal slices $W_{::1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\ 1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $W_{::2}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\ 0&0\end{pmatrix}$:
&nbsp;&nbsp;(i) argue $\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{W})\neq1$;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(ii) assume $\mathbf{W}=\mathbf{a}_1\circledast\mathbf{b}_1\circledast\mathbf{c}_1+\mathbf{a}_2\circledast\mathbf{b}_2\circledast\mathbf{c}_2$ with $M_r=\mathbf{a}_r\mathbf{b}_r^{\top}$; the slice space of $\mathbf{W}$ is then $\{\alpha M_1+\beta M_2\}$, and a $2\times2$ computation with $M_r=\mathbf{a}_r\mathbf{b}_r^{\top}$ gives $\det(\alpha M_1+\beta M_2)=\kappa\,\alpha\beta$ with $\kappa=[\mathbf{a}_1,\mathbf{a}_2][\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2]$ (where $[\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y}]=x_1y_2-x_2y_1$); explain why this rules out $\kappa=0$ and why the pencil must therefore contain exactly two singular directions, while the slice pencil $\alpha W_{::1}+\beta W_{::2}=\begin{pmatrix}\beta&\alpha\\ \alpha&0\end{pmatrix}$ of $\mathbf{W}$ has $\det=-\alpha^2$ and only one;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(iii) conclude $\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{W})=3$ (write it explicitly as a sum of three rank-$1$ tensors) and explain why $\mathbf{W}$, padded into the $50\times40\times30$ cube, lies in the Tucker format with core $(2,2,2)$ but outside every rank-$2$ CP format. (3 P.)

d) An archive budget allows exactly $1\%$ of the raw storage for a $200\times200\times200$ tensor. How many numbers is that, and how many bytes would the raw tensor have occupied as `float64`? Find the largest rank of a CP representation that fits the budget, and the largest rank $r$ of a *cubical* Tucker format (core $r\times r\times r$, factors $200\times r$) that fits; verify both answers against the neighbouring rank. What is the trade-off between the two winners in one sentence? (1 P.)
