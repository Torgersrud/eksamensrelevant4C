### **Problem 3** (Tangent plane to a surface, 8 points)

Consider the surface $S$ given by

$$S=\left\{(x,y,z)^{\top}\in\mathbb{R}^{3}\ :\ x^{3}+2xyz+x\cos(z^{2})=2\right\}.$$

Find an equation for the tangent plane at the point $\mathbf{p}=(1,\ -1,\ 0)^{\top}\in S$.
